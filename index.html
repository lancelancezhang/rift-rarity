    About Rift Rarity
    
    
    
      Rift Rarity is a free browser game inspired by rarity-dive trivia.
      You get League of Legends prompts, a short timer on each one, and score by how rare your valid answer is.
      Common picks stay near the surface. Obscure correct picks sink you toward Challenger depth.
    
    
    
    
      Play for fun, share your depth without revealing answers, and try to beat your friends.
      No install required. Works on desktop and mobile.
